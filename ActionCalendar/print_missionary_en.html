💻 Digital Missionary: 30-Day Guide

Please print this page or save it as a PDF. Connect with God and leave beautiful footsteps in the digital world.

🟢 Week 1: Clarifying Your Digital Calling
6/1 (Mon・Clarifying Your Digital Calling)
Quiet yourself before God. Ask Him to open your spiritual eyes to see the unmet gospel needs in this digital generation.
6/2 (Tue・Clarifying Your Digital Calling)
Consider the content creation side: "influencers, making videos, designing graphics, regular posting." Is this what God is prompting you to do?
6/3 (Wed・Clarifying Your Digital Calling)
Consider the community side: "building groups, caring for and pastoring online seekers." Do you have a burden to pastor lost souls?
6/4 (Thu・Clarifying Your Digital Calling)
Consider the education side: "writing and designing Sunday school materials tailored for the digital generation." Do you have a passion for translating truth?
6/5 (Fri・Clarifying Your Digital Calling)
Consider the technical side: "building church apps, improving administrative efficiency." Are you willing to dedicate your professional skills to God's kingdom?
6/6 (Sat・Clarifying Your Digital Calling)
Open the "Digital Ministry Prayer Cloud." Do you see a vision in the keywords? Ask God to inspire you with new ways to communicate the faith.
6/7 (Sun・Clarifying Your Digital Calling)
During Sunday rest, entrust all the possibilities you thought about this week to the Lord, asking Him to personally point out the path for you.
🟡 Week 2: Organizing Your Digital Resume
6/8 (Mon・Organizing Your Digital Resume)
Write down three digital skills or software tools you are best at (e.g., video editing, copywriting, programming).
6/9 (Tue・Organizing Your Digital Resume)
Write down one digital skill you possess but haven't mastered yet, but are very interested in learning. Pray for this passion to learn.
6/10 (Wed・Organizing Your Digital Resume)
Review your past work or ministry experiences. What "secular" professional skills can actually be used for the Lord?
6/11 (Thu・Organizing Your Digital Resume)
Pray about the areas where you feel inadequate. Acknowledge that we cannot do everything, and learn to humbly look to God's provision.
6/12 (Fri・Organizing Your Digital Resume)
Pray for the brothers and sisters of GBC. Ask God to personally connect members with different gifts to form a strong team.
6/13 (Sat・Organizing Your Digital Resume)
Think about how you can actively get to know other members who have a burden for digital missions, preparing your heart for future teamwork.
6/14 (Sun・Organizing Your Digital Resume)
Present your organized "Digital Resume" to God in prayer, declaring that all these abilities will point to God's will.
🔵 Week 3: Trying New AI Tools
6/15 (Mon・Trying New AI Tools)
Decide on one AI tool you want to explore this week (like ChatGPT, Midjourney, Notion AI, etc.) and register an account.
6/16 (Tue・Trying New AI Tools)
Spend 15 minutes watching a beginner tutorial video for that AI tool to understand its basic functions and use cases.
6/17 (Wed・Trying New AI Tools)
Get hands-on! Try using the tool to generate a short testimony draft, an accompanying image, or organize a devotional note.
6/18 (Thu・Trying New AI Tools)
Think about how this tool could be applied to church ministry or personal evangelism? Write down one specific idea.
6/19 (Fri・Trying New AI Tools)
Share your experience of trying the new tool these past few days with a friend, sparking each other's imagination for digital missions.
6/20 (Sat・Trying New AI Tools)
Evaluate whether this tool is suitable for continued, in-depth learning. If so, plan out your next learning steps.
6/21 (Sun・Trying New AI Tools)
Thank God for providing the wisdom of technology. Ask God to help us use tools well, becoming vessels to transmit love and truth, rather than being controlled by the tools.
🔴 Week 4: Leaving Time and Space for the Lord
6/22 (Mon・Leaving Time and Space for the Lord)
Be honest with yourself: Do I often feel "too busy to serve"? Repent to God for this busyness stuffed by the world.
6/23 (Tue・Leaving Time and Space for the Lord)
Review your weekly calendar. Find out what time is wasted on meaningless phone scrolling or binge-watching.
6/24 (Wed・Leaving Time and Space for the Lord)
Try to intentionally set aside 15 minutes every day doing nothing but waiting on God, completely sanctifying this time.
6/25 (Thu・Leaving Time and Space for the Lord)
Ask God to give us the wisdom to refuse unnecessary social events or trivial matters, leaving more time in our lives for God.
6/26 (Fri・Leaving Time and Space for the Lord)
Pray for God to adjust our values: "It's not about stuffing our time full and then saying we can't work for God; it's about offering our time to God first."
6/27 (Sat・Leaving Time and Space for the Lord)
Formulate a specific and feasible "micro-service" plan, such as spending one hour a week on online caring or making gospel image cards.
6/28 (Sun・Leaving Time and Space for the Lord)
With a heart of repentance and renewal, hand the sovereignty back to God, declaring that your time and life are ready to be used by the Lord.
🏁 Conclusion and Continuation
6/29 (Mon・The Action Continues)
Look back over these thirty days. What clear vision and inspiration has God given you regarding the "Digital Missionary" calling? Write it down.
6/30 (Tue・The Action Continues)
Pray for the digital ministries that Grace Baptist Church is about to launch. Declare that you are prepared and ready to respond to God's sending at any time!
