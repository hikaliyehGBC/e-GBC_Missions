💡 One-Month Digital Light Action Plan (Action Plan: Light)
This guide is designed to help you practice the "Salt and Light" project on digital social platforms (FB, LINE, Threads) between June 1 (Monday) and June 30 (Tuesday). Through the micro-habit formation of "same-day themes and regular check-ins," let's use our fingertips to block the algorithms of anger and spread genuine goodness online.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📅 Daily Light Blueprint
To lower the barrier to execution, we will practice the same salt and light exercise on the same day every week. As the weeks progress, our impact will gradually expand:
Monday: [Fingertip Defense] When seeing controversial or emotionally triggering content, absolutely do not comment, do not like, do not argue—quietly detour.
Tuesday: [Trash Cleanup] Block or hide 1 account/fan page that purely incites division, actively cleaning your digital environment.
Wednesday: [Positive Likes] Actively vote with your likes for 3 posts on your feed that carry warmth, positive energy, or value.
Thursday: [Warm Comments] Leave a sincere, specific, and edifying comment of encouragement under a post from a friend or fellow believer.
Friday: [Sending Gratitude] Post a short sharing of gratitude on social media (e.g., a small blessing you encountered today).
Saturday: [Real Life] Share an unfiltered photo of your physical life, testifying to the true joy and companionship outside of algorithms.
Sunday: [Card Forwarding] Forward a beautifully crafted comforting scripture card from the church to 1 friend in need, sending beautiful blessings from your fingertips.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📝 30-Day Gradual Checklist
🟢 Week 1: Small Steps to Set Sail (6/1 - 6/7) ── Daily Difficulty: ★
Focus: Control your fingertips, focusing on basic defense and digital environment cleanup.
[ ] 6/1 (Mon・Fingertip Defense): When seeing sensational news or trash posts on Threads that incite division, absolutely do not click—scroll right past them.
[ ] 6/2 (Tue・Trash Cleanup): Block or unfollow 3 fan pages or accounts that frequently post negative energy or bitter remarks.
[ ] 6/3 (Wed・Positive Likes): Actively like 3 posts from friends on your feed that are warm, joyful, or full of positive energy, giving them your most direct support.
[ ] 6/4 (Thu・Warm Comments): Leave a sincere, simple greeting under a good friend's post sharing their daily life (e.g., "Seeing your post brightened my day!").
[ ] 6/5 (Fri・Sending Gratitude): Share on social media (or LINE timeline) 1 small blessing you encountered today that you are thankful for, spreading a glimmer of positivity.
[ ] 6/6 (Sat・Real Life): Share an unfiltered photo of beautiful nature you took on a walk, and write a sentence thanking God for His creation.
[ ] 6/7 (Sun・Card Forwarding): Share an article you believe is highly worth reading by more people, and thoughtfully write down your reason for recommending it.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🟡 Week 2: Solidifying Habits (6/8 - 6/14) ── Daily Difficulty: ★★
Focus: Turn your defense into muscle memory, and actively use likes and comments to "vote" for a river of goodness.
[ ] 6/8 (Mon・Fingertip Defense): When encountering others loudly arguing or publicly shaming someone in the comment sections of Threads or Facebook, insist on not leaving a single word of sarcasm.
[ ] 6/9 (Tue・Trash Cleanup): Actively leave a LINE trash group that frequently spreads political gossip, conspiracy theories, or emotional flame wars.
[ ] 6/10 (Wed・Positive Likes): Actively vote with your likes for 3 posts on your feed that carry positive energy, value, or professional knowledge.
[ ] 6/11 (Thu・Warm Comments): Leave a specific and edifying encouragement under a friend's post (e.g., "Thank you for sharing, this really inspired me today!").
[ ] 6/12 (Fri・Sending Gratitude): Share a wise quote you recently read from a physical book or devotional, along with a one-sentence reflection of your own.
[ ] 6/13 (Sat・Real Life): Share about the most loving couple or family relationship you've seen at church, refusing to let gender conflicts and family drama dominate social media narratives.
[ ] 6/14 (Sun・Card Forwarding): Pick an image or text that conveys spiritual rest and share it to your parents' or work group, adding: "A gift for everyone who worked hard this week."
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔵 Week 3: Deep Practice (6/15 - 6/21) ── Daily Difficulty: ★★★
Focus: Actively radiate light, building a warm and edifying digital footprint on social media.
[ ] 6/15 (Mon・Fingertip Defense): When receiving unverified gossip or fake news in a group, refuse to casually forward it. Use your calmness to block the spread of rumors.
[ ] 6/16 (Tue・Trash Cleanup): When seeing ads or fake accounts purely inciting division, don't comment or argue. Simply click "Report and Block" to actively clean your digital environment.
[ ] 6/17 (Wed・Positive Likes): Actively like posts from 3 friends who rarely get attention but consistently try to share their daily lives or ministries, becoming their silent supporter.
[ ] 6/18 (Thu・Warm Comments): Actively send a private message on social media to a coworker who is currently under heavy pressure from work, studies, or ministry, expressing your sincere appreciation and care.
[ ] 6/19 (Fri・Sending Gratitude): Share a link to high-quality music or a worship song that brings peace and rest to your soul, and write about how it accompanied you through a low point.
[ ] 6/20 (Sat・Real Life): Share how you responded to workplace difficulties with goodness and love, refusing to let the false strongholds of division built by Satan shape societal values.
[ ] 6/21 (Sun・Card Forwarding): Forward and recommend a video channel or artist that makes you feel hopeful, and thoughtfully write down your reason for recommending them.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔴 Week 4: Life Impact (6/22 - 6/28) ── Daily Difficulty: ★★★★
Focus: Let your fingertips become a conduit for the gospel, improving the online environment with goodness.
[ ] 6/22 (Mon・Fingertip Defense): Enjoy your fourth week of turning off non-interpersonal push notifications. When facing sensitive social controversies, if you must speak, insist on only using words that promote peace and understanding.
[ ] 6/23 (Tue・Trash Cleanup): Assist your children, family, or elders around you, teaching them how to block scams and hide trash posts, becoming stewards of their digital environment.
[ ] 6/24 (Wed・Positive Likes): Actively like and comment on posts from high-quality Christian media and gospel creators you see, supporting them in the digital harvest field with practical actions.
[ ] 6/25 (Thu・Warm Comments): Actively check on a friend who posted words of depression or emptiness on social media. Send a warm private message of intercession and greeting—no preaching, just listening.
[ ] 6/26 (Fri・Sending Gratitude): Share your grateful takeaways from practicing "Digital Sabbath" over the past month, testifying how God renewed you when you quieted down.
[ ] 6/27 (Sat・Real Life): Share the 3 most joyful, lingering offline moments you experienced in your physical life this month, influencing those around you to return to real life.
[ ] 6/28 (Sun・Card Forwarding): Read Isaiah 52:7. Use AI to create an image card and forward it to your groups, sharing the joy of being salt and light and being used by God in the digital world this month.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🏁 Conclusion and Continuation (6/29 - 6/30)
[ ] 6/29 (Mon・Final Fingertip Review): Looking at your peaceful, edifying comments on the internet over the past month, thank God for guarding your tongue and fingertips.
[ ] 6/30 (Tue・Trash Cleanup Cont.): Write down your takeaways from these 30 days of the Digital Light Action, and give thanks in prayer: "By relying on the Lord, I have been salt and light in the fingertip world, leaving the most beautiful footsteps!"
