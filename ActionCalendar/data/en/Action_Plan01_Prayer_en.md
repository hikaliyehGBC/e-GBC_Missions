⛪ One-Month Digital Prayer Action Plan (Action Plan: Prayer)
This guide is specially designed for the "One-Month Digital Prayer Action" initiated after the Grace Baptist Church Missions Conference. From June 1 (Monday) to June 30 (Tuesday), we will use the behavioral principle of "same-day themes, gradual progression" to build an altar for digital missions on our knees, using short, clear, and unforgettable daily steps.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📅 Daily Theme Blueprint
To lower the barrier to execution, we will pray for the same theme on the same day every week. As the weeks progress, our prayer time will gradually deepen:
Monday: [Ministry Watch] Pray for the digital ministries and pastors/coworkers generated at the GBC Missions Conference.
Tuesday: [Pray for Nations] Scan to draw a nation/Taiwan region, read the materials and videos, and pray for that place.
Wednesday: [Hearts of this Generation] Pray for the hearts of this generation deeply affected by social media anxiety (youth FOMO vs. adult information fatigue).
Thursday: [Media Environment] Pray for the online environment of Taiwan's top 3 platforms (FB, LINE, Threads) and Christian media creators.
Friday: [Digital Missionaries] Pray for the coworker groups committing to AI web practicals and their equipping.
Saturday: [Repentance] Review your digital footprint for the week, and ask God to cleanse the fleshly reactions from mindlessly forwarding or leaving bitter comments.
Sunday: [Sabbath Rest] Turn off devices and quiet your heart before service, praying for the congregation's Sunday reception and spiritual rest.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📝 30-Day Gradual Checklist
🟢 Week 1: Small Steps to Set Sail (6/1 - 6/7) ── 3 mins daily
Focus: Extremely low barrier, aiming to turn the habit of picking up our phones into a habit of kneeling in prayer.
[ ] 6/1 (Mon・Ministry Watch): Pray for God's protection over the sprouting vision of digital missions from the recently concluded conference, that the congregation's hearts won't grow cold.
[ ] 6/2 (Tue・Pray for Nations): Enter the missions conference page, click "Pray for Taiwan," watch the promo video, and pray for the local needs.
[ ] 6/3 (Wed・Hearts of this Gen): Pray for your own heavy use of 3C devices, emotionally triggered outbursts, and hijacked attention that robs your rest. Ask God for true peace free from condemnation.
[ ] 6/4 (Thu・Media Environment): Pray for the online environment of the top three platforms (FB, LINE, Threads) you scrolled through today. Ask God to keep your heart from dancing with anger.
[ ] 6/5 (Fri・Digital Missionaries): Pray for Christians laboring in Taiwan's online media. Ask God to bless the work of their hands to edify more people in need.
[ ] 6/6 (Sat・Repentance): Close your eyes for 1 minute and review your phone usage this week. Ask God to wash away the irritation accumulated from passively reading unedifying gossip.
[ ] 6/7 (Sun・Sabbath Rest): 10 minutes before the service begins, turn off your phone or put it on silent in your bag. Quietly pray for the congregation beside you to find rest as you worship together.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🟡 Week 2: Solidifying Habits (6/8 - 6/14) ── 5 mins daily
Focus: Establish a fixed prayer time and rhythm, deepening the content of each theme.
[ ] 6/8 (Mon・Ministry Watch): Pray specifically for the preparation, technology, and coworkers of the highly anticipated "Digital Ministry Prayer Cloud."
[ ] 6/9 (Tue・Pray for Nations): Enter the missions conference page, click "Pray for Nations," watch the promo, and spend 3 minutes praying for the local mission needs and the peace of missionary families.
[ ] 6/10 (Wed・Hearts of this Gen): Pray especially for youths under 30 who are kidnapped by the perfect filters of IG and short videos. Ask God to grant them the security of unconditional love and acceptance.
[ ] 6/11 (Thu・Media Environment): Pray for the "invisible seekers" looking for answers about faith on digital platforms. May God's true light guide them through online content.
[ ] 6/12 (Fri・Digital Missionaries): Pray that GBC can raise up more digital professionals to serve, using their gifts for God to expand the borders of His kingdom.
[ ] 6/13 (Sat・Repentance): Reflect on whether you casually forwarded unverified fake news or gossip in LINE groups this week, and ask God for forgiveness.
[ ] 6/14 (Sun・Sabbath Rest): During Sunday service, pray for the speaker's words and the listening hearts of the congregation. May God's word be a shield for everyone facing Monday's challenges.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔵 Week 3: Deep Practice (6/15 - 6/21) ── 8 mins daily
Focus: Enter substantive missions and harvest field intercession, firing on all cylinders in prayer.
[ ] 6/15 (Mon・Ministry Watch): Pray for the most "liked" items on the "Digital Ministry Prayer Cloud." Ask God to raise up coworkers who can drive these ministries forward.
[ ] 6/16 (Tue・Pray for Nations): Click "Pray for Taiwan." For the administrative district drawn today, find a local church on Google Maps and pray for that church to be salt and light in its community.
[ ] 6/17 (Wed・Hearts of this Gen): Pray especially for adults over 40 who are mentally exhausted by the daily bombardment of LINE group messages and polarized public opinions. Ask God to grant them calm.
[ ] 6/18 (Thu・Media Environment): Pray for Christian media KOLs and video creators who are influential online. Ask God to protect their spiritual lives and families.
[ ] 6/19 (Fri・Digital Missionaries): Pray for the expansion of your own digital skills. If you've ever had a burning desire to learn more, ask God to ignite it so you can be used by Him.
[ ] 6/20 (Sat・Repentance): Facing the fleshly reactions and judgments that may have arisen from online public opinions or group debates this week, ask for Jesus' gentle reset in your prayers.
[ ] 6/21 (Sun・Sabbath Rest): Pray that God will help us completely put down work-related LINE messages on Sunday, enjoying the holy day of rest face-to-face with God and brothers and sisters.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔴 Week 4: Life Impact (6/22 - 6/28) ── 10 mins daily
Focus: Declare beautiful footsteps at our fingertips, letting the impact of prayer extend outward.
[ ] 6/22 (Mon・Ministry Watch): Thank God for the inspirations and images that have surfaced in your mind regarding "digital missions" this month. Believe that the God who does new things will make a way for GBC.
[ ] 6/23 (Tue・Pray for Nations): Click "Pray for Nations." For the country drawn today, find its capital on Google Maps and declare that God's kingship will be manifested there.
[ ] 6/24 (Wed・Hearts of this Gen): Pray for the young generation in Taiwan collectively pouring out their anxiety and emptiness on Threads. Ask God to raise up more young believers who can accompany them.
[ ] 6/25 (Thu・Media Environment): Bless all gospel posts and faith cards that convey truth and comfort online. Declare that these contents will shine like a glimmer of light in the dark night.
[ ] 6/26 (Fri・Digital Missionaries): Pray for all members whose hearts are passionate about digital missions. Ask God to remove the obstacles before them and supply them abundantly, making them willing to accept GBC's digital ministry calling.
[ ] 6/27 (Sat・Repentance): Review your social media comments over the past month. Thank God for guarding our tongues and fingertips, and declare that we have exited the game of keyboard wars.
[ ] 6/28 (Sun・Sabbath Rest): Read Isaiah 52:7. Pray for the invisible seekers all over Taiwan who are looking for spiritual peace online. Declare that the beautiful footsteps of the gospel will unfold in the digital harvest field.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🏁 Conclusion and Continuation (6/29 - 6/30)
[ ] 6/29 (Mon・Ministry Watch): Pray for God to open a way for the sustainable operation of GBC's digital ministries and the recruitment of a new batch of coworkers.
[ ] 6/30 (Tue・Kingdom Come): Thank God for sitting as King over the digital virtual world. Give thanks for how God has adjusted your spiritual vision over these thirty days.
