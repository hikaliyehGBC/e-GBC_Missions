🌳 One-Month Digital Sabbath Action Plan (Action Plan: Sabbath)
This guide aims to help you build healthy and safe boundaries with your phone between June 1 (Monday) and June 30 (Tuesday). We are not advocating a "complete ban on phones," but rather, through the behavioral science of "same-day themes and regular check-ins," we want to help your brain learn how to rest properly after using your phone and regain the Zone 2 capital of joy in real life.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📅 Daily Sabbath Blueprint
To minimize resistance, we will practice the same Sabbath exercise on the same day every week. As the weeks progress, the difficulty and depth will gradually increase:
Monday: [Environment Cleanup] Check app push notifications and choose three to turn off, reducing active interruptions.
Tuesday: [Cool Down & Discharge] Try spending 5 minutes connecting with nature/plants/animals (e.g., looking at balcony plants, hugging a pet, looking at the sky) after scrolling on your phone.
Wednesday: [Physical Monotasking] When studying or working, challenge yourself to place your phone face down and avoid playing background videos, practicing distraction-free monotasking.
Thursday: [Table Sabbath] Try putting away your phone or turning off your computer screen during lunch or dinner, focusing on enjoying the food and conversation with those around you.
Friday: [Nighttime Power Off] Turn off all glowing screens for the last 30 minutes before bed, resetting your brain's dopamine with reading, stretching, or listening to music.
Saturday: [Healthy Body] Schedule a 30-minute to 1-hour "completely phone-free walk/run" to accumulate Zone 2 real-life joy capital.
Sunday: [Physical Bible] "Pick up a physical Bible to read; do not use your phone to read scripture." Practice screen-free worship and deep focus for your soul.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📝 30-Day Gradual Checklist
🟢 Week 1: Small Steps to Set Sail (6/1 - 6/7) ── Daily Difficulty: ★
Focus: Build physical barriers, clear environmental noise, and take the first step.
[ ] 6/1 (Mon・Environment Cleanup): Check your phone's app push notifications and pick three to turn off (e.g., shopping, games, or news likes/alerts) to reduce active interruptions.
[ ] 6/2 (Tue・Cool Down & Discharge): Today, every time you finish using a 3C device, force yourself to look away and gaze at green plants, balcony potted plants, or the sky outside your window for 1 minute.
[ ] 6/3 (Wed・Physical Monotasking): During one of your work or study sessions today (e.g., 30 minutes), put your phone face down or avoid having videos playing in the background.
[ ] 6/4 (Thu・Table Sabbath): During your lunch break today, put your phone on silent or turn off your computer screen, and focus entirely on savoring the taste of your food.
[ ] 6/5 (Fri・Nighttime Power Off): One hour before bed, turn off the lights in your room, and turn off or cover extra light sources to allow for a more peaceful sleep.
[ ] 6/6 (Sat・Healthy Body): Go for a 20-minute jog or brisk walk, and as you do, pray and praise God for the joy you experienced this week.
[ ] 6/7 (Sun・Physical Bible): Pick up a physical Bible for Sunday reading. Avoid using a phone app for scripture, and experience the focused warmth of flipping through paper pages.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🟡 Week 2: Solidifying Habits (6/8 - 6/14) ── Daily Difficulty: ★★
Focus: Reinforce the brain's Reset mechanism after phone use through fixed actions and times.
[ ] 6/8 (Mon・Environment Cleanup): Open your YouTube or social media platforms and unfollow accounts you haven't watched in a long time.
[ ] 6/9 (Tue・Cool Down & Discharge): Whenever you scroll on your phone continuously for over 15 minutes, immediately walk to the balcony or yard after putting it down. Spend 5 minutes connecting with plants/animals or observing the sky.
[ ] 6/10 (Wed・Physical Monotasking): Place visible sticky notes in the spaces where you use 3C devices the most: "Focus, don't be distracted," "Remember to discharge after using 3C."
[ ] 6/11 (Thu・Table Sabbath): During dinner today, when eating with family or roommates, have everyone put their phones in a basket by the table and focus on face-to-face conversation.
[ ] 6/12 (Fri・Nighttime Power Off): Turn off glowing screens for the last 30 minutes before bed. Replace scrolling with reading a physical book, writing a journal, or stretching.
[ ] 6/13 (Sat・Healthy Body): Go for a 30-minute jog or brisk walk, and as you do, pray and praise God for the joy you experienced this week.
[ ] 6/14 (Sun・Physical Bible): Do not use your phone during Sunday service. Prepare paper or a notebook to handwrite this week's verses and the message you received today.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔵 Week 3: Deep Practice (6/15 - 6/21) ── Daily Difficulty: ★★★
Focus: Deep digital fasting to accumulate high-quality real-life capital.
[ ] 6/15 (Mon・Environment Cleanup): Check your phone's "Screen Time" and set a strict "45-minute daily limit" for your most used social media app.
[ ] 6/16 (Tue・Cool Down & Discharge): After scrolling on your phone today, interact with a pet, go to a park to watch squirrels and birds, or walk barefoot on the grass for 5 minutes to rebuild your brain's sensory connection with the real world.
[ ] 6/17 (Wed・Physical Monotasking): For 45 minutes while reading a book or writing a report today, challenge yourself to "monotask" and absolutely do not scroll on your phone while doing other things.
[ ] 6/18 (Thu・Table Sabbath): For the first five bites of your meal today, try to mindfully chew 30 times before swallowing. After the meal, recall if you've eaten 15 different types of vegetables this week.
[ ] 6/19 (Fri・Nighttime Power Off): Try a "45-minute pre-sleep power off." Completely move your phone charger out of the bedroom, switch to a physical alarm clock, and enjoy high-quality, peaceful sleep.
[ ] 6/20 (Sat・Healthy Body): Schedule an afternoon to wander in a nearby park. Find at least 10 different wild flowers on the park grass and take pictures of them.
[ ] 6/21 (Sun・Physical Bible): Today on Sunday, from the worship service to small group Bible study, use a physical Bible the entire time. Do not open your phone's Bible app at all, focusing on the direct presence of God's word.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔴 Week 4: Life Impact (6/22 - 6/28) ── Daily Difficulty: ★★★★
Focus: Sabbath has been internalized as a lifestyle; declare that your soul has found true freedom in Christ.
[ ] 6/22 (Mon・Environment Cleanup): Open your YouTube or social media platforms. Pick 3-5 items from the top 20 recommended posts on your homepage and select "Not interested."
[ ] 6/23 (Tue・Cool Down & Discharge): 5 minutes of nature contact after using 3C devices has become muscle memory. Feel the significant improvement in your focus and mental calmness after resetting your brain.
[ ] 6/24 (Wed・Physical Monotasking): Share your experiences and feelings with a friend about focusing on real life and not being distracted by phones/3C devices this past month.
[ ] 6/25 (Thu・Table Sabbath): Enjoy the deliciousness of every meal without phone interference. Recall your happiest meal this month and pray with gratitude for a wonderful dining mood.
[ ] 6/26 (Fri・Nighttime Power Off): Enjoy the grace of a full month of pre-sleep power offs. Feel the dramatic improvement in your sleep quality and how refreshed you feel when waking up in the morning.
[ ] 6/27 (Sat・Healthy Body): Challenge yourself to get up and walk for 20 minutes within 10 minutes after a meal. Research confirms that light cardio after meals stabilizes blood sugar and prevents fat accumulation.
[ ] 6/28 (Sun・Physical Bible): Pick up your physical Bible and read a Psalm. Thank God for resetting your restless soul through the physical Bible over the past month.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🏁 Conclusion and Continuation (6/29 - 6/30)
[ ] 6/29 (Mon・Fat on the Path): Check your daily average screen time for June. Looking at the decreased numbers, thank God for reclaiming the sovereignty of your time.
[ ] 6/30 (Tue・Return Attention to God): At the end of the 30 days, walk up to a large tree, take a deep breath, and declare to God: "Algorithms no longer dictate my mood, because my soul enjoys true Sabbath rest in Christ!"
