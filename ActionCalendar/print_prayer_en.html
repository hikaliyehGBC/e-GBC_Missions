Pray for your own heavy use of 3C devices, emotionally triggered outbursts, and hijacked attention that robs your rest. Ask God for true peace free from condemnation.

Pray for the online environment of the top three platforms (FB, LINE, Threads) you scrolled through today. Ask God to keep your heart from dancing with anger.

10 minutes before the service begins, turn off your phone or put it on silent in your bag. Quietly pray for the congregation beside you to find rest as you worship together.
